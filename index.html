FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Read the documentation or talk to us.

Which text-to-speech model should I use?

Kugel 3.5 is the default: it gets short replies, IPA pronunciation, pauses and spelled-out codes right far more often than Kugel 3, at 75% of its price. Turbo (kugel-2-turbo) costs less for high volumes but has no IPA notations and is less precise on numbers and codes.

Does my data leave Europe?

No. The models are built, trained and hosted in the EU. With the EU Sovereign Cloud — included on every plan for EU/EEA customers — hosting is done by EU companies only, and no provider with a US parent processes your audio, text or voice references.

Do you train on my content?

Never. Your audio, text and voice references are not used to train our models.

Which frameworks do you support?

Official plugins for LiveKit Agents and Pipecat, built-in endpoints for Vapi and Cognigy, and an MCP server for Claude and Cursor. SDKs for Python and TypeScript, plus REST and WebSocket streaming for any other pipeline.

Can I run the models on my own hardware?

Yes, on Enterprise. The models run in your data centre, behind your firewall, with the same SDK and API as the hosted service — so your integration doesn't change.
