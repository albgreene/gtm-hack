    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Can't find what you need? Write to us.

    Will callers notice they're talking to an AI?

    Our voices are among the most natural in Europe, with sub-300ms latency and natural turn-taking. For transparency we recommend — and by default enable — a short disclosure at the start of each call.

    How does KugelAudio track leads and KPIs?

    Each call is transcribed and analyzed in real time. The agent extracts structured fields (contact, intent, budget, timeline), assigns a lead score and writes everything to your dashboard and CRM. KPIs like answer rate, conversion, handle time and sentiment update live.

    What happens when a call needs a human?

    You define the handover rules — by intent, lead score, keyword or caller. The agent transfers warm, with a spoken summary to your colleague and the full transcript in their inbox.

    Is it really GDPR-compliant?

    Yes. KugelAudio GmbH is a German company. Models are developed and hosted in the EU, we sign a DPA with every customer, and retention periods are fully configurable. Self-hosting is available on Enterprise.

    How long does setup take?

    Most customers go live within 3–5 working days. Our team helps write the call flow, connect your number and integrate your CRM.
